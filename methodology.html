Estimated monthly payments

Cards sum fixed-rate amortizing mortgage principal + interest, reported annual taxes divided by 12, and normalized monthly HOA/common charges. Defaults: 20% down, 30 years, 6.71% annual interest, from Freddie Mac's September 3, 2026 benchmark. These are editable device-local assumptions, not personalized lending quotes or approval. Changing the term does not automatically substitute that term's market rate. The calculation is P × r / (1 − (1 + r)−n), with monthly rate r and monthly payment count n; zero interest uses P / n.

Missing price, tax or HOA produces a clearly labeled known subtotal, never a complete estimate. Tax reported above 4% of asking price annually is withheld from the estimate as a possible source outlier—not silently corrected. Reported annual taxes below 0.2% of price are flagged for possible abatements or incomplete assessments; these are screening thresholds, not tax rules. Taxes may rise or be reassessed after purchase. Known garage charges and garage taxes at the rechecked Summit penthouse are included and labeled; one known $119.38/month assessment is separately displayed with a total including it.

The requested mortgage + tax + HOA estimate excludes insurance, PMI, maintenance, utilities, closing costs and special assessments unless separately displayed. Less than 20% down may require PMI. Confirm current fees, abatements and any assessments with the managing agent and lender. CFPB: principal/interest versus the full monthly payment. Monthly-payment sorting puts incomplete estimates last.

Listing facts, costs, schools and commute

  • Every refreshed card links to its individual source listing and names the listing brokerage and MLS identifier where supplied. Price, availability, bed/bath counts, area, build year, tax year and school names are source-reported, not independently inspected. Attached single-family records are grouped as townhouses/attached; condominium ownership remains in Condos.
  • Annual property taxes are divided by 12. HOA/common charges use explicitly supplied fees and frequency. A condo without a reported fee remains unknown even if its association flag is false. Taxes and fees exclude mortgage, insurance, utilities, assessments and closing costs. The carrying-cost sort is HOA plus tax only, with incomplete totals last. Low fees are a preference, not a hard exclusion; missing fees need follow-up.
  • Among 500 active homes, 499 have a build year, 493 a tax amount, 452 a known HOA/common-charge amount, and 463 with at least one named school (467 have a school or district name; “Contact Agent” placeholders are excluded). Schools are listing-agent assignments, not verified enrollment eligibility or quality ratings. The preserved Best schools filter only uses original 4/5+ screening estimates, explicitly unverified. New unrated homes are not presumed worse. Verify directly with the school district.
  • Build year and condition are separate. A newer home is not automatically in better condition. Condition is source-reported where available; otherwise it requires inspection. Original fit scores and rail-minute estimates are retained only as unverified prior estimates; no new ratings or journey times were invented.
  • Nearest rail is measured from MLS address coordinates to official MTA subway stations and MTA railroad stations (LIRR), retrieved September 6. Distances are straight-line, not walking routes or station entrances. Across the 501 refreshed records, 497 locations are address points and four are street-interpolated. Check transfers, schedules, entrances and first/last-mile travel.

Bikeability — proximity is only one indicator

NYC: distance to the nearest current Class I (protected/off-street) segment in the NYC DOT Bicycle Routes dataset, network snapshot July 24, 2026. This covers all 359 active NYC homes. Distance is to the line segment, not necessarily an accessible entrance. A nearby segment may be disconnected from your desired route.

Nassau: distance to a mapped separated cycleway/track or bicycle-designated path in © OpenStreetMap contributors, snapshot September 6, 2026. Each card links to the closest mapped way. We queried the bounding box 40.57, −73.80, 40.88, −73.49 for highway=cycleway, cycleway/left/right=track, or highway=path with bicycle=designated; excluded explicit private/no access and no/private/dismount bicycle tags. Mapping may omit facilities, and a path may be unpaved. An unmarked restriction or unavailable entrance can change access. This is a different source and classification from NYC, not a comparable network-quality score.

The quarter-mile filter and bike-distance sort use straight-line proximity to these mapped facilities. They do not establish a safe, connected or comfortable trip. Check pavement, hills, traffic stress, crossings, route continuity, storage, lighting and actual access in person. Derived Nassau proximity data is attributed to OpenStreetMap and available under the Open Database License (ODbL 1.0); this does not relicense independent MLS or government data.

Likes and learning

Private shared likes, reason tags and notes still sync between your devices. The new Bikeability and Crime safety tags let you explicitly state why you like a home. The taste profile finds common attributes and explains similarity recommendations; these are observable patterns, not a trained model or certainty about your preferences. Bike proximity can influence recommendations when you explicitly choose Bikeability. Crime counts never become a safety score. New recommendations exclude records marked inactive or unverified.

Photo cards and negative feedback — September 7 update

500 homes have source-linked listing-photo previews (up to six per card). All primary image links returned image responses when checked September 7. Photos are loaded from the listing provider, not copied or generated. The 33 remaining homes have no verified photo and are labeled accordingly. Additional structured details across 501 homes come from their September 6 listing snapshots; they are not newly verified availability or inspected conditions. Building and lot features may be shared amenities for condos.

The catalogue stays public on GitHub Pages. A shared-password login protects synced feedback; a device remembers its session for 180 days unless signed out or browser storage is cleared. Either person can select Kartik or Minoli. The password is checked on the server and is not included in the public page.

Dislikes are explicit and undoable, with multiple reason choices and notes. Removing a like does not mean disliking the home. Rejected homes are excluded from recommendations for the selected profile. Price, size, known HOA/tax, rail distance and mapped bike proximity can produce explained score reductions when they repeat an explicitly rejected drawback. Size comparisons now stay within the same property type; a rejected house does not establish a minimum condo size. Rail-distance caution requires matching measurement methods and at least half a mile; a straight-line distance is not a walking route or commute. Source-supported interior/light claims can receive modest weight when explicitly requested in positive feedback, with explanations labeled as listing reports. Subjective layout, appearance and safety are not inferred from other numbers. Positive and negative feedback remains private to the shared login.
